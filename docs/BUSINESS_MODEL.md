# Gummy OS Business Model

## Principle

The personal computer is the adoption surface. Governance is the commercial product.

Gummy OS should not need to extract rent from every consumer Gummy, model message, Bowl, Link, or application. Individuals can run a compatible Gummy OS edition, choose their intelligence, keep local data, export their objects, and build independent compatible editions.

## Paying customers

### Software vendors

Pay for Application Pack design, capability and workflow mapping, verification harnesses, compatibility certification, signed distribution, model/version regression testing, support, and incident investigation.

### Enterprises

Pay for Actor identity and role control, approved Molds, policy engine, model and connector brokers, Pack registry, runtime pools, audit and retention, dedicated or sovereign deployment, administrative support, SLA, and long-term maintenance.

### OEM and branded editions

Pay for branded distributions, preconfigured Actors, Molds, applications, Packs, models, policies, embedded vertical workflows, compatibility rights, and support.

## Free personal edition

The intended free baseline includes:

- desktop shell;
- local Gummy object space;
- personal Actor identity;
- portable Mold;
- basic Bowls, Links, and Grabs after the Social Layer ships;
- local or user-funded models;
- basic Pack execution after the governed-app phase;
- import/export;
- local Action Receipts;
- self-hosting and compatible-edition rights subject to the final license.

Hosted storage, managed inference, or third-party services may carry their actual usage cost without changing the free-software principle.

## Commercial moat

The moat is the accumulated trust system:

- Application Pack standards;
- vendor relationships;
- verification corpus;
- Actor and Mold verification patterns;
- policy templates;
- runtime compatibility knowledge;
- signed registries;
- enterprise deployment expertise;
- audit evidence;
- protocol brand and certification;
- a personal computer vendors want to reach.

## Anti-patterns

Gummy OS should not become:

- a pay-to-play application pyramid;
- an advertising identity graph;
- a captive model storefront;
- a marketplace that prevents direct vendor relationships;
- an engagement-only social feed;
- a celebrity or character impersonation market;
- a cloud computer that holds export hostage;
- a system where a Mold's appearance substitutes for Actor verification.
