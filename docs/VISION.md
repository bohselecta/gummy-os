# Gummy OS Vision

## The proposition

A person should be able to open a URL, enter full screen, and arrive inside a complete creative computer where files, projects, conversations, applications, Actors, Agents, and shared work become understandable as one place.

Gummy OS is not one workflow and not one forced interface. It is a playful WebOS medium with explicit authority, containment, provenance, and optional connection to native intelligence.

## The visible product

```text
Gummy OS       platform
Gummy Canvas   where the person makes and explores
Gummy Bar      candy-store system bar
Glopper        gummy-candy companion
Glopper Panel  conversation and control without replacing the Canvas
```

The Gummy Bar is full of candy icons. They may visually represent applications, Actors, Gummies, Bowls, tasks, controls, and Glopper, while the underlying protocol objects remain distinct.

Glopper should feel friendly, funny, alive, and inviting—the successor to assistants like Clippy without becoming intrusive or pretending to possess authority.

Hayden supplies the final Glopper character, Gummy logo, and visual vibe.

## What changes beneath the familiar computer

- Actors persist at stable `@addresses`;
- Agents remain separate replaceable intelligence;
- Humans remain in Master Control;
- Molds define how a Human or Agent may operate an Actor;
- Glopper is available across the Canvas through the Gummy Bar;
- Gummies preserve ownership, provenance, and rights;
- recursive creation and composition are allowed;
- authority never inherits automatically;
- consequential work and boundary crossings leave evidence;
- host and enterprise security stay where they already work.

## One Glopper, multiple execution places

From the Human's perspective, Glopper is one companion.

Underneath, Glopper may route work among:

```text
agent:glopper-web
agent:glopper-cloud
agent:glopper-native
agent:glopper-phone
```

Each executor remains separately identifiable, permissioned, leased, and receipted. A local executor may use Ollama, llama.cpp, an IDE, local directories, or device capabilities only when explicitly granted. Web Glopper remains independently useful for Gummy OS, web tools, GitHub, cloud development, and connected services.

## Existing Glopper lineage

The existing `bohselecta/glopper` application is already a local-first process director for multi-agent build workflows. It is the first substantial native expression of Glopper's future role, especially around state, memory, gates, handoffs, and evidence.

Its real v1.0 identity remains intact while its capabilities can later become part of the broader Glopper companion.

## The playground

Gummy OS provides primitives rather than prescriptions:

- Gummy Canvas;
- Gummy Bar;
- Actors;
- Agents;
- Molds;
- Master Control;
- Gummies;
- Bowls;
- Links;
- Grabs;
- applications and mini-apps;
- protocols and runtimes;
- Receipts.

The hexagonal interface remains an optional mini-app. Other people may build studios, games, worlds, dashboards, social rooms, professional tools, blank Canvases, or interfaces not yet imagined.

Play is onboarding. People discover power by making, combining, opening, talking, dragging, sharing, and exploring.

## Recursive future

Humans, Actors, and Agents may create Actors, Agents, Gummies, tools, worlds, and composed surfaces.

> **Every new Actor or Agent receives independent identity, provenance, authority, disclosure, and revocation. Creation never transfers power automatically.**

## Security opportunity

Host, native OS, enterprise identity, network controls, endpoint protection, biometrics, passkeys, and hardware security continue protecting the device and connection.

Gummy OS adds a contained layer:

- web content remains in browser/capsule boundaries;
- downloads begin as quarantined Gummies;
- native promotion is explicit;
- suspicious workspaces can be reset or burned;
- approved results and Receipts can survive disposal;
- every bridge is permissioned and receipted.

This reduces blast radius without claiming that a browser or Linux automatically solves security.

## Portable life

Gummy OS can open from an ordinary browser, phone, PWA, governed WebView, or future live-USB/native environment.

The product name remains Gummy OS across devices. The Canvas and Bar adapt to screen size instead of creating separate Desktop and Web brands.

## Immediate product

```text
open Gummy OS in Ubuntu browser
→ enter Gummy Canvas
→ choose candy icons from Gummy Bar
→ open Glopper Panel
→ open personal Actor
→ import real source Gummy
→ authorize agent:glopper-web through a bounded Mold
→ Master Control shows location, scope, and data flow
→ receive result Gummy without changing source
→ inspect complete Receipt
→ revoke Agent/Mold
→ prove quarantine and burn/reset
→ return to the same understandable state
```

Only after that proof does the team inspect the already-built native distro and connect `agent:glopper-native`.

## Opportunity

The personal playground can remain useful and free because commercial value lies in trusted connection and governance:

- verified native bridges;
- Application Packs and certification;
- Agent/connector brokers;
- enterprise policy and audit;
- secure runtime pools;
- quarantine, promotion, and evidence systems;
- private, dedicated, sovereign, live-USB, OEM, and vertical deployment;
- compatibility testing and support.

## Terminal reality

A person opens Gummy OS.

Their Actor is already there. The Gummy Bar looks like a candy store. Glopper is ready without taking over the Canvas. Their Gummies, applications, worlds, Bowls, Links, projects, and tools remain where they left them.

They can work directly, talk to Glopper, bind an Agent through a Mold, open another Actor, combine selected things into a new surface, or build an interface nobody else imagined.

The system tells them what is local, what is web, what is synchronized, which Agent is acting, what authority it has, and what happened.

> **Open Gummy OS. Pick a candy. Make a world.**
