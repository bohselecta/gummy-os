The demonstrated operating loop was:

```text
Actor
→ Intent
→ Command Center / operational visibility
→ Production
→ Studios / Agents
→ Artifacts / Gummies
→ Returns / Receipts
→ Distribution direction
```

The demonstration is evidence of the implemented architecture and of the natural Human usage patterns becoming visible through use.

## 4. Concepts explained verbally or inferred from the demonstration

The founder’s follow-up identified these concepts as important enough to preserve canonically:

### Actor presence

- a Human-owned Actor can be Human-live, Human-available, AI-represented, static, offline, or dormant;
- an authorized AI representative is not the Human;
- Actor identity persists while operator and runtime may change;
- many Actor and group windows may remain simultaneously open or minimized.

### Saved social configurations

- groups can be saved as persistent instances rather than disposable chats;
- a saved instance retains identity, membership, state, history, permissions, purpose, linked projects, and visual layout;
- reopening the instance restores a social topology rather than creating an empty room.

### Shared Vision and Production formation

- group conversation can become the origin record for a Shared Vision;
- the system may propose saving the idea, estimating it, inviting contributors, or forming a Production;
- the conversation becomes provenance without automatically becoming public or executable.

### Shared compute and contribution

- participants may share estimated compute or service costs;
- new contributors may reduce proposed future shares;
- contribution includes creative work, Assets, labor, expertise, distribution, and compute—not money alone;
- contribution, credit, ownership, control, and revenue are separate agreement dimensions.

### Command Center and Receipts

- the system should show current Productions, Agents, Work Orders, Receipts, costs, decisions, Returns, and blocked items;
- Receipts make Agent behavior inspectable rather than magical;
- Command Center exposes attention and evidence while Master Control remains the authority layer.

### Distribution and continuing presence

- Radio/AfterCast can transform approved source packages into podcasts, aftershows, serial programs, or private audio Receipts;
- Channels can carry creator media, premieres, watch groups, and Family Room activity;
- an Actor can build a persistent approved body of work and continue public presence under explicit representation and publication rules.
