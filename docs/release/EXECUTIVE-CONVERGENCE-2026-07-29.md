## Product outcome

Gummy Box is the Human-owned workspace for durable sources, projects,
results, history, imports, backup, and connections. Composer is the goal-first
visual proposal surface over existing Productions, Actors, Places, Gummies,
Actor Plans, review gates, and destinations. The convergence pass keeps the
complete product architecture and makes canonical objects easier to continue,
arrange, inspect, and apply without granting execution authority.

The cleanup restores an explicit `executed: false` contract across every
exported Composer mutation and denial path. It also makes canonical references,
Human Actor restoration, applied Actor Plan evidence, and result-card browser
tests deterministic without weakening the production CSP or hiding legitimate
provenance.

## Architecture boundaries

- The Human retains authority.
- Actor and Agent identities remain distinct.
- Composer remains proposal-only.
- Master Control remains the authority surface.
- Make Production remains the sole Production-wide execution transition.
- A Composer edit cannot start a Run, grant authority, call a provider, charge
  money, accept a result, publish, mutate a frozen Run, or silently change
  Actor-global defaults.
- Completion is not Human acceptance.
- Acceptance is not publication.
- Phase 17 live MCP execution remains disabled; Issue #36 stays open.
